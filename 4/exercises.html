Choose a Muscle Group

Pick the area of the body you want to train. Next, you will narrow it down to one specific muscle.

  • Chest

    Presses and flyes for the upper, mid, inner, and lower chest.

  • Back

    Pulls and rows for your lats, upper back, lower back, and traps.

  • Arms

    Curls, extensions, and grip work for the biceps, triceps, forearms, and brachialis.

  • Shoulders

    Presses and raises for all three delt heads plus the rotator cuff.

  • Legs

    Squats, hinges, and raises for the quads, glutes, hamstrings, and calves.
