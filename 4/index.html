Pick a muscle. Get the exercises.

Muscle Map shows you which exercises target which muscle, how to do them with proper form, and what equipment you need. It doesn't have meal plans or a social feed, it just answers what targets what.

Why Muscle Map?

Anyone who has walked into a gym for the first time knows the feeling of standing in front of dozens of machines and having no idea what any of them actually work. Strength training is one of the best things you can do for your health, but it only works if it is done with a plan.

Structured Training

Structured strength training means knowing what you are training and why. Following a plan instead of wandering from machine to machine is what turns time in the gym into real results.

Targeting Specific Muscles

Every muscle has movements that train it best. When you know which exercise hits which muscle, you can bring up weak points, balance your physique, and keep making progress.

Less Information Overload

Most fitness apps put workout tracking, macro counters, meal plans, and social feeds all in one place. Muscle Map only answers one question: what exercise targets what muscle, and how do I do it right?

About Me

Lifting is one of the things I am most passionate about in life. I work out every day, and I love giving other people fitness advice whenever they ask for it.

I built Muscle Map because people kept asking me the same questions, like what to do for their chest or what a certain machine works. This site puts all of those answers in one place.
