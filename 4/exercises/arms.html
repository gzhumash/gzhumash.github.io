Choose a Arms Muscle

Curls, extensions, and grip work for the biceps, triceps, forearms, and brachialis.

← Back to all muscle groups

  • Biceps

    The front of the upper arm, responsible for bending the elbow.

  • Triceps

    The back of the upper arm and the biggest part of arm size.

  • Forearms

    The muscles below the elbow that control your wrist and grip.

  • Brachialis

    The muscle under the biceps that pushes it up and adds arm thickness.
