Brachialis

The muscle under the biceps that pushes it up and adds arm thickness.

Hammer Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells

How To Do It

  1. Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides with a neutral (palms facing in) grip.
  2. Keeping your elbows tucked at your sides, curl both dumbbells up at the same time toward your shoulders.
  3. Squeeze at the top without letting your elbows drift forward.
  4. Slowly lower both dumbbells back to the starting position.

Cross-Body Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells

How To Do It

  1. Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides with a neutral (palms facing in) grip.
  2. Keeping your upper arm still, curl one dumbbell up and across your body toward the opposite shoulder.
  3. Squeeze at the top, then slowly lower the dumbbell back to your side.
  4. Alternate arms with each repetition.
