Forearms

The muscles below the elbow that control your wrist and grip.

Seated Barbell Wrist Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Flat bench
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Load the bar and sit on a bench with your forearms resting on your thighs and your wrists just past your knees.
  2. Hold the bar with a supinated (palms up) grip.
  3. Using only your wrists, curl the bar up toward your body.
  4. Once your forearm flexors are fully contracted, slowly lower the weight back to the starting position.

Reverse Cable Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Cable tower
  • EZ bar attachment

How To Do It

  1. Clip the EZ bar attachment to the bottom pulley of the cable machine and select the desired weight.
  2. Grab the bar with a pronated (palms down) grip, hands about shoulder-width apart, and stand tall facing the tower with your arms straight.
  3. Keeping your elbows tucked at your sides, curl the bar up toward yourself until your forearms are close to your upper arms.
  4. Slowly lower the bar back to the starting position under control.

Cable Forearm Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Cable tower
  • Straight bar attachment

How To Do It

  1. Clip the straight bar attachment to the top pulley of the cable machine.
  2. Grab the bar with palms facing down, keeping the bar in front of your head, with your biceps pressed against your forearms.
  3. Face the tower, step back, and hinge slightly at the hips. Keep your elbows tucked to your sides and shoulders relaxed.
  4. Curl just your hands toward the floor, driving your knuckles straight down. Squeeze hard at the bottom.
  5. Control the weight on the way up.
