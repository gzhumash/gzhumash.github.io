Biceps

The front of the upper arm, responsible for bending the elbow.

EZ Bar Preacher Curls

Equipment Needed

  • EZ bar or barbell
  • Preacher bench

How To Do It

  1. Set the seat so that when you sit down, the top of the angled pad fits snugly right into your armpits.
  2. Place the back of your upper arms flat against the pad, keeping your elbows fully supported and aligned with your shoulders.
  3. Grip an EZ-bar on the inner angled grooves using an underhand grip (palms up), keeping your wrists straight and rigid.
  4. Pull the weight upward toward your shoulders by flexing your elbows, keeping your back flat and your upper arms pressed into the pad.
  5. Slowly lower the bar back down until your arms are almost fully extended, maintaining constant tension on your biceps.

Standing Barbell Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell or EZ bar
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Grasp a barbell or EZ bar with an underhand grip and stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  2. Keeping your elbows at your sides, curl the weight up as far as possible.
  3. Squeeze your biceps at the top, then slowly lower the weight back to the starting position.
