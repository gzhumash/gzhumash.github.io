Triceps

The back of the upper arm and the biggest part of arm size.

EZ Bar Skullcrushers

Equipment Needed

  • EZ bar
  • Flat bench
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Sit on the edge of a flat bench holding an EZ bar, then lie back while keeping the bar close to your chest.
  2. Press the bar to lockout above your chest.
  3. Unlock your elbows and lower the bar toward your forehead (or just above it), keeping your upper arms still.
  4. Once your forearms reach parallel or just below, extend your elbows and flex your triceps to press back to lockout.

Cable Tricep Pushdowns

Equipment Needed

  • Cable tower
  • EZ bar attachment

How To Do It

  1. Clip the EZ-bar attachment to the top pulley of the cable machine.
  2. Grab the outer, downward-sloping angles of the EZ-bar with palms facing down.
  3. Face the tower, step back, and hinge forward slightly at the hips. Tuck your elbows tight against your ribs, and pull your shoulder blades down and back.
  4. Keep your upper arms locked, press the bar down towards your thighs by extending your elbows, fully locking out your arms and squeeze your triceps.
  5. Slowly allow the bar to rise back up until your forearms are just past parallel to the floor, stopping before your elbows drift forward or the weight stack touches.
