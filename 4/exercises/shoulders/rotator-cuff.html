Rotator Cuff

Small stabilizing muscles that keep the shoulder joint healthy.

Wall Ball Rolls

Equipment Needed

  • Small ball (stability, tennis, or soft medicine ball)
  • Wall

How To Do It

  1. Stand facing a wall and press a small ball against it with one hand at about shoulder height.
  2. Keep your arm straight and apply light pressure to hold the ball in place.
  3. Roll the ball in small, controlled circles, moving from your shoulder.
  4. Perform circles in both directions, then switch arms.
