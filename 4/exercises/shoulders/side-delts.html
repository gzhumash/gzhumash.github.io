Side Delts

The outer shoulder that makes your shoulders look wider.

Dumbbell Lateral Raises

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells

How To Do It

  1. Select your dumbbells and step into an open area, holding them at your sides with a neutral grip.
  2. Keeping a slight bend in your elbows, take a deep breath and raise the dumbbells out to the sides up to shoulder height.
  3. Slowly lower the dumbbells back to the starting position under control.

Cable Lateral Raises

Equipment Needed

  • Cable machine
  • Single-grip handle

How To Do It

  1. Set a pulley to the lowest position, attach a single-grip handle, and stand side-on to the machine.
  2. Grab the handle with your far hand so the cable runs across the front of your body.
  3. Keeping a slight bend in your elbow, raise your arm out to the side until it reaches shoulder height.
  4. Slowly lower the handle back to the starting position, then switch sides once you finish the set.
