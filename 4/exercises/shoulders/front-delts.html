Front Delts

The front of the shoulder, worked in almost every pressing movement.

Overhead Barbell Presses

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Squat rack
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Set the barbell in a rack just below shoulder height and load the desired weight.
  2. Grip the bar with a pronated grip at (or just outside) shoulder width, step under it, and unrack it with a neutral spine.
  3. Take two steps back, set a shoulder-width stance, inhale, and brace your core.
  4. Tuck your chin and press the bar to lockout overhead, exhaling at the top.
  5. Slowly lower the bar under control back to your upper chest.

Seated Dumbbell Presses

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells
  • Adjustable bench

How To Do It

  1. Set an adjustable bench to 90 degrees, pick up the dumbbells, and sit with the ends resting on your knees.
  2. Kick your knees up one at a time to bring each dumbbell to shoulder height, then rotate your palms to face forward.
  3. Take a deep breath and press the dumbbells overhead by extending your elbows.
  4. Slowly lower the dumbbells until your elbows are at roughly 90 degrees (or slightly lower).
