Rear Delts

The back of the shoulder, important for posture and a balanced look.

Cable Rear Delt Flies

Equipment Needed

  • Cable crossover machine

How To Do It

  1. Position dual cable pulleys at shoulder height, standing in the center.
  2. Hold the left cable with your right hand and the right cable with your left hand.
  3. Step back to create tension, keep a slight bend in your elbows, and engage your core.
  4. Pull your arms out and back in a wide arc at shoulder level, leading with your elbows.
  5. Squeeze your rear delts at the peak, then slowly return to the start.

Cable Face Pulls

Equipment Needed

  • Cable machine
  • 2x D-handle attachments

How To Do It

  1. Attach both handles to a cable pulley set at about face height.
  2. Grasp both with your palms facing each other and step back into a split stance with your arms straight out in front of you.
  3. Inhale and pull the handles toward your face, pulling your elbows behind your head.
  4. Slowly return the weight to the starting position under control.
