Lower Chest

The lower pec fibers, hit with decline and dip movements.

Decline Bench Presses

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Decline bench with rack
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Lie on a decline bench, hook your feet under the pad, and set your hands just outside shoulder width.
  2. Pinch your shoulder blades together and drive them into the bench.
  3. Take a deep breath and unrack the bar (with a spotter's help if needed), keeping your upper back tight.
  4. Unlock your elbows and slowly lower the bar in a straight line until it touches just below your sternum.
  5. Press the bar back up in a straight line by driving yourself into the bench and extending your elbows.

Chest Dips

Equipment Needed

  • Dip station (parallel bars)

How To Do It

  1. Step up onto the dip station and grip the handles with a neutral grip, arms locked out.
  2. Lean your torso slightly forward to keep the emphasis on your chest.
  3. Unlock your elbows and slowly lower your body until your upper arms are roughly parallel with the floor.
  4. Push through your palms to drive back up to the starting position.

High-to-Low Cable Flies

Equipment Needed

  • Cable crossover machine
  • 2 single-grip handles

How To Do It

  1. Set both pulleys to the highest position, attach single-grip handles, and select the desired weight.
  2. Grasp the handles with a slightly pronated (palms down) grip and step forward into a split stance.
  3. Bending your elbows up at a 90 degree angle and close to your torso, push the handles in front of you and slightly down.
  4. Squeeze your chest as the handles meet below chest height.
  5. Slowly release the handles back to the starting position under control.
