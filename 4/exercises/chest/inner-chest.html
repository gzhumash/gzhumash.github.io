Inner Chest

The area near the sternum, trained by squeezing the hands together.

Hex Presses

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells
  • Flat bench

How To Do It

  1. Sit on a flat bench with a dumbbell in each hand, lie back, and hold them above your chest with a neutral grip.
  2. Press the dumbbells firmly together so they touch along their full length.
  3. Keeping them squeezed together, slowly lower them until they lightly touch your mid-chest.
  4. Press them back up to lockout while continuing to squeeze them together.

Machine Flies (Pec Deck)

Equipment Needed

  • Chest Fly Machine

How To Do It

  1. Adjust the seat so the handles sit directly at mid-to-upper chest height.
  2. Sit flat against the pad, pinch your shoulder blades back and down, and keep your chest puffed out.
  3. Move your arms inward in a wide hugging arc, focusing on bringing your inner elbows and biceps together.
  4. At the center, lock out your arms almost completely and aggressively squeeze your chest for 1 to 2 seconds to maximize tension.
