Quads

The front of the thigh, used to straighten the knee.

Barbell Back Squats

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Squat rack
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Set the barbell in a rack just below shoulder height and load the desired weight.
  2. Step under the bar and rest it across the muscles of your upper back (not your neck), gripping it wide for stability.
  3. Brace your core, stand up to unrack the bar, and take a small step back with your feet about shoulder-width apart.
  4. Keeping your eyes forward and chest up, sit your hips back and down until your thighs are parallel with the floor.
  5. Drive through your heels to stand back up without forcefully locking out your knees.

Leg Presses

Equipment Needed

  • Leg press machine
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Load the machine, sit down, and place your feet on the platform in a shoulder-width stance.
  2. Take a deep breath, extend your legs, and release the safeties.
  3. Lower the weight under control until your knees are bent to about 90 degrees or slightly below.
  4. Drive the platform back up by extending your knees without forcefully locking them out.
  5. Re-engage the safeties when your set is finished.

Leg Extensions

Equipment Needed

  • Leg extension machine

How To Do It

  1. Select the weight and adjust the seat so your knees line up with the machine's pivot point.
  2. Sit down and position your shins behind the lower pad.
  3. Take a deep breath and extend your legs, flexing your quads and exhaling as your knees straighten.
  4. Slowly lower your feet back to the starting position.

Bulgarian Split Squats

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells
  • Flat bench

How To Do It

  1. Hold dumbbells at your sides with a neutral grip and stand about a stride in front of a bench.
  2. Place the top of your back foot on the bench behind you.
  3. Bend your front knee and lower yourself until your back knee almost touches the ground beneath your hip.
  4. Drive through your front foot to return to the starting position, then switch legs after the set.
