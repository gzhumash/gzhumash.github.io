Calves

The lower leg muscles that point your toes and push you off the ground.

Standing Calf Raises

Equipment Needed

  • Standing calf raise machine

How To Do It

  1. Adjust the shoulder pads to your height and step underneath them.
  2. Place the balls of your feet on the platform with your toes pointed forward and your heels hanging off.
  3. Stand up tall to lift the weight, then lower your heels until your calves are fully stretched.
  4. Push through the balls of your feet to raise your heels as high as possible, exhaling as you flex your calves.

Seated Calf Raises

Equipment Needed

  • Seated calf raise machine

How To Do It

  1. Sit down and adjust the knee pads to rest on your quads, your heels will naturally hang off.
  2. Extend your ankles and release the safety bar.
  3. Lower the heels by dorsiflexing the ankles until the calves are fully stretched.
  4. Extend the ankles and exhale as you flex the calves.
