Glutes

The muscles of the hips and the strongest in the body.

Barbell Hip Thrusts

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Flat bench
  • Weight plates
  • Bar pad (optional)

How To Do It

  1. Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench and roll a barbell over your legs to your hip crease (a bar pad adds comfort).
  2. Plant your feet flat on the floor about hip-width apart with your knees bent.
  3. Drive through your feet and squeeze your glutes to lift your hips until your torso is parallel with the floor.
  4. Slowly lower your hips back to the starting position.

Cable Kickbacks

Equipment Needed

  • Cable tower
  • Ankle wrap attachment

How To Do It

  1. Set the pulley at the bottom and attach your ankle wrap to it.
  2. Step back with the attached leg and cross your free leg over the cable.
  3. Pull the attached leg back at a 45 degree angle without bending your knees until it's parallel to the ground.
  4. Slowly lower your leg down while keeping it straight and return to starting position.

Romanian Deadlifts

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Squat rack
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Position the bar over the top of your shoelaces and assume a hip width stance.
  2. Assume a double overhand grip just outside of hip width and deadlift the weight into position at the top with the hips and knees locked out.
  3. Begin the RDL by pushing your hips back and hinging forward until the bar is just below knee height.
  4. Return to the starting position by driving through the whole foot and focus on pushing the floor away.
