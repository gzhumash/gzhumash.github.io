Hamstrings

The back of the thigh, used to bend the knee and hinge at the hip.

Stiff Leg Deadlifts

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over your shoelaces.
  2. Hinge at the hips with only a slight knee bend and grasp the bar with a shoulder-width, double overhand grip.
  3. With a neutral spine, drive through your whole foot and extend your hips to stand up, keeping the bar close to your legs.
  4. Push your hips back and hinge forward, keeping your legs nearly straight, to lower the bar back to the floor.
  5. Reset your position before the next rep.

Seated Leg Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Seated leg curl machine

How To Do It

  1. Select the weight and adjust the pad so it rests just above the back of your heels.
  2. Tense your hamstrings to lift the weight slightly off the stack.
  3. Take a deep breath and curl the pad down and back as far as possible, keeping your spine neutral.
  4. Slowly return the weight to the starting position.

Lying Leg Curls

Equipment Needed

  • Lying leg curl machine

How To Do It

  1. Select the weight and adjust the pad so it rests just above the back of your heels.
  2. Tense your hamstrings to lift the weight slightly off the stack.
  3. Take a deep breath and curl the pad up and toward your back as far as possible, keeping your spine neutral.
  4. Slowly return the weight to the starting position.
