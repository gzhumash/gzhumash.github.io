Traps

The muscles from your neck to mid back that shrug and stabilize the shoulders.

Barbell Upright Rows

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell or EZ bar
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and hold a barbell in front of your thighs with a shoulder-width, overhand grip.
  2. Stand tall with your chest up and shoulders back.
  3. Leading with your elbows, lift the bar straight up along your body to about chest level, keeping your elbows higher than your wrists.
  4. Slowly lower the bar back to the starting position under control.

Dumbbell Shrugs

Equipment Needed

  • Dumbbells

How To Do It

  1. Stand with a dumbbell on each side of your body, hinge down, and grab them with a neutral grip.
  2. Stand up tall with your spine neutral and your arms hanging at your sides.
  3. Contract your traps to raise your shoulders straight up toward your ears, squeezing hard at the top.
  4. Slowly lower the dumbbells back to the starting position.
