Lats

The wide muscles along your sides that give the back its width.

Lat Pulldowns

Equipment Needed

  • Lat pulldown machine
  • Wide-grip bar

How To Do It

  1. Attach a wide-grip bar to the lat pulldown machine and sit down with your thighs secured under the pads.
  2. Grasp the bar with a pronated (double overhand) grip.
  3. Depress your shoulder blades, then pull the bar toward your upper chest by driving your elbows down.
  4. Once your elbows are in line with your torso, slowly return the bar to the starting position under control.

Wide Grip Pull-Ups

Equipment Needed

  • Pull-up bar

How To Do It

  1. Grasp the pull-up bar with a pronated grip, wider than shoulder width.
  2. Take a deep breath, squeeze your glutes, and brace your abs.
  3. Depress your shoulder blades, then drive your elbows down toward the floor to pull your chin up to the bar.
  4. Once your lats are fully contracted, slowly lower yourself back to the starting position.

Straight Arm Lat Pulldowns

Equipment Needed

  • Cable machine
  • Wide-grip bar

How To Do It

  1. Attach a wide-grip bar to a high cable pulley and stand facing the machine.
  2. Grasp the bar with a pronated grip at roughly shoulder width and hinge slightly forward at the hips.
  3. Keeping a slight bend in your elbows, depress your shoulder blades and pull the bar down in an arc toward your thighs.
  4. Once your lats are fully contracted, slowly let the bar return to the starting position under control.
