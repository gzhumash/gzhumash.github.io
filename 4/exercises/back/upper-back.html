Upper Back / Rhomboids

The muscles between your shoulder blades that pull them together.

Bent Over Rows

Equipment Needed

  • Barbell
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Stand holding a barbell with a double overhand grip.
  2. Hinge forward at the hips until your torso is roughly parallel with the floor (or slightly above), keeping your spine neutral.
  3. Drive your elbows behind your body and retract your shoulder blades, pulling the bar toward your belly button.
  4. Once the bar touches your body, slowly lower it back to the starting position under control.

Seated Cable Rows

Equipment Needed

  • Seated cable row machine
  • V-bar or close-grip handle

How To Do It

  1. Attach a V-bar to the seated row machine, select the weight, and sit with your feet on the platform.
  2. Grasp the handle with a neutral grip and, with your knees slightly bent, sit upright with your back straight and shoulders back.
  3. Keeping your torso still, pull the handle into your stomach.
  4. Squeeze your shoulder blades together, pause, then slowly return the weight to the starting position.

Machine T-Bar Rows

Equipment Needed

  • Chest-supported T-bar row machine
  • Weight plates

How To Do It

  1. Lie face down on the pad of the T-bar machine and grasp the handles with a neutral or pronated grip.
  2. Drive your elbows behind your body while retracting your shoulder blades.
  3. Pull until your elbows reach (or just pass) your torso.
  4. Slowly lower the weight back to the starting position under control.
