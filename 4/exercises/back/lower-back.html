Lower Back / Erectors

The muscles along your spine that keep your torso upright.

Back Hyper Extensions

Equipment Needed

  • 45-degree back extension bench (Roman chair)
  • Weight plate (optional)

How To Do It

  1. Adjust the pad of the back extension bench so it sits just below your hip crease, then hook your ankles under the foot pads.
  2. Cross your arms over your chest (or hold a weight plate for extra resistance) and keep your spine neutral.
  3. Hinge at the hips and slowly lower your torso toward the floor until you feel a stretch in your hamstrings.
  4. Contract your lower back and glutes to raise your torso until your body forms a straight line with your legs.
