Kazuma Kiryu

Description

Kazuma Kiryu is the main protagonist of all six mainline Yakuza games, known as the "Dragon of Dojima".

Being adopted by Kazama, head of the Dojima family, Kiryu grows into being a member of the Tojo Clan Yakuza.

After being blamed for a murder, he gets expelled from the family and rebels against it to fight for justice for his mentor and friends.

Why do I like him?

  • Strength - Kiryu is super duper strong. Throughout the game, Kiryu has to beat up armies (20-30) of thugs at once.
  • Morals - he has a very strong sense of justice, and always helps the weak and stands by his word, friends, and family.
  • Determination - nothing can break his spirit, and even on the verge of death, he keeps it real.
